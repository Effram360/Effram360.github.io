Research

Engineer and perturb

I change what cells make and how their proteins are modified, using CRISPR knockouts, glycoengineered cell lines, and enzyme and inhibitor treatments.

Analytical Chemistry 2026 · Cell Systems 2026 · Cell Commun. Signal. (in revision)

Measure

I read out what changed by mass spectrometry, from glycoproteomics and phosphoproteomics in engineered cells to immunopeptidomics, plasma proteomics and proteogenomics in patient samples.

Nature Cancer (reviewed) · Cell Press (in review)

Model

I built PTMlink, a database that scores how glycosylation and phosphorylation sites vary together across human tumors. Other projects include multi-modal integration of omics data with VAE architectures and fine-tuning protein language models to predict PTM sites.

bioRxiv (preprint)

Ultimately, I am interested in predictive models of cell state that account for protein modifications, and in the experiments needed to build them. My goal is to help match patients with the most suitable and effective therapeutics for their disease.
